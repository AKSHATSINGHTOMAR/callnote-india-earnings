The first half of the year 27 has been our strongest ever half-yearly performance with robust growth across key financial and operational parameters. In the second quarter, Rossell Techsys reported its highest ever quarterly revenue of 185.56 crores, representing a growth of 47 percent over the corresponding quarter of the previous year. We also achieved an EBITDA of 29 crores and our highest ever quarterly profit before tax of 12.91 crores, making the first time in the company's history that quarterly PBT has crossed double digits. The revenue after the first six months is 345 crores with an EBITDA of 52 crores and a profit before tax of 22.3 crores. We are particularly pleased and encouraged by the progress we are making in the semiconductor segment. Based on our ongoing customer interactions, qualification progress, and visibility into future opportunities, we expect semiconductor revenues to scale meaningfully over the coming quarters and years. With increasing global investments in semiconductor manufacturing, technological advancements, and the diversification of global supply chains, we see significant opportunities for Rossell Techsys to continue expanding its presence in the sector. Turning to operational performance, momentum continues to remain strong across our business segments. During the first half of the financial year, the company secured fresh orders with approximately 430 crores and submitted bids aggregating approximately 1,350 crores, reflecting the healthy pipeline of opportunities across aerospace and defense, semiconductors, space, and industrial sectors. I am also pleased to share that Rossell Techsys was recently recognized as one of the best manufacturing brands by ED Edge. This prestigious recognition is a testament to our continued commitment to manufacturing excellence, innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction. It is also a reflection of the hard work and dedication of our entire team. During the period, we also celebrated an important milestone in our company's journey, 15 years of Rossell Techsys. It was a special occasion for us all, providing an opportunity to reflect on how far we have come, and more importantly, reaffirm our ambitions for the future. As part of these celebrations, we inaugurated our new facility within the aerospace park. This additional facility, taken on lease, comprises of approximately 140,000 square feet, with an option to expand to 210,000 square feet, and is expected to commence early next quarter. The facility will provide significant additional manufacturing capacity to support the strong growth opportunities emerging from the semiconductor and other non-defense sectors while creating substantial headroom for future business wins. Equally encouraging is a continued improvement in our working capital efficiency. Despite operating at a significantly larger scale, inventory carrying levels have improved from 7.98 months in quarter to the financial year 26 to 6.67 months in the second quarter of financial year 27. This reflects stronger planning, better procurement practices, tighter execution controls, and a continued focus on operational excellence. Improving working capital efficiency will remain an important priority as we continue to scale the business. While financial performance remains important, we firmly believe that our people are the true foundation of long-term value creation. During the period, we conducted our all-hands meeting, where we shared our long-term vision, growth roadmap, and strategic opportunities and priorities with employees across the organization. In recognition of the dedication and contribution of our employees towards this remarkable phase of growth, the company also announced a special bonus. This reflects our belief that the success of the organization must be shared with the people who make it possible. Our workforce continues to expand in line with business requirements, and we ended the period with the total employee strength of 1352 professionals, representing one of our strongest competitive advantages as we continue to scale across multiple sectors and geographies. Looking ahead, we remain highly optimistic about the opportunities before us. Historically, the third and fourth quarters have always been the strongest quarters for Rossell Techsys, and we entered the second half of this financial year with considerable confidence and positive momentum. With a strong audiobook, increasing customer demand, expanding manufacturing capabilities, and continued improvements in operational efficiencies, we are encouraged by the growth opportunities ahead and expect to build further on the momentum achieved during the first half. Our aerospace and defense business continues to benefit from strong global demand, increasing defense spending, and the ongoing realignment of global supply chains. At the same time, our semiconductor business is opening up substantial new opportunities, giving Rossell Techsys an additional and highly promising engine of growth. What is particularly satisfying is that we have achieved these results while continuing to invest significantly in our people, infrastructure systems, and future capacity. These investments are not simply to support the business we have today, but to build the Rossell Techsys of tomorrow. We believe we are still in the early stages of the much larger growth journey, with a diversified customer base, expanding capabilities, a strong balance sheet, growing institutional confidence, and an exceptional team. We believe Rossell Techsys is well positioned to capitalize on the opportunities ahead. While we remain focused on discipline, execution, and mindful of the challenges, our ambition remains clear to build Rossell Techsys into a significantly larger, globally respected manufacturing and technology company. I would like to thank our customers, employees, shareholders, and partners for their continued trust and support.
