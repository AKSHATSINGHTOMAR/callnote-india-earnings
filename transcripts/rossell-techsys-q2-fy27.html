Equally encouraging is a continued improvement in our working capital efficiency. Improving working capital efficiency will remain an important priority as we continue to scale the business. This reflects our belief that the success of the organization must be shared with the people who make it possible. Looking ahead, we remain highly optimistic about the opportunities before us. These investments are not simply to support the business we have today, but to build the Rossell Techsys of tomorrow. We believe Rossell Techsys is well positioned to capitalize on the opportunities ahead.
