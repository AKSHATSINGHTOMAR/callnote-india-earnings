This has been the strongest half in our history. In quarter two, revenue was INR 1,746 crores, up 89.9% year-on-year. EBITDA was INR 307 crores, up 177%, with a margin of 17.6%. PAT before the exceptional item was INR 220 crores, up 214%. For the first half year, revenue was INR 3,148 crores, up 89.7%. EBITDA was INR 515 crores at a 16.3 margin. PAT before the exceptional item was INR 362 crores, up 252%. Also, sequentially, quarter two revenue grew 24.5% over quarter one, and EBITDA margin rose from 14.8% to 17.6%. The loss is fully covered by insurance, and the claim has already been lodged. Excluding it, the underlying business delivered the INR 220 crores PAT, which I just mentioned. CP PLUS remains the core engine of this growth. It contributed about 88% of revenue in quarter two against 86% a year ago. Within CP PLUS, IP products are now about 79% of the portfolio, up from 71%. Our leadership in the Indian video surveillance has widened. In the 12 months to September 2026, our share of the total market, including the unorganized segment, was about 42%, which at the time of our IPO was 30%. The industry's move to higher data security standards as well as trusted supply chains played to our strengths. Cybersecurity-certified products needed scale in manufacturing, depth in R&D, and a secure supply of critical components. They needed the nationwide reach to put products in the market across India and an Indian brand which the Indian consumers can trust. They also needed the most experienced management and field teams in the Indian surveillance ecosystem. We had all these ingredients in place, and the gains eventually came from our team's execution. We expect to keep gaining market share through the rest of the year. On our revised guidance, our FY2027 share is expected to be in the range of 43% to 45%. On the margin side, our gross margin for the first half was 30.4%, up 374 basis points year-onyear. Three things drove it: a richer brand and product mix, deeper localization initiatives, and operating leverage. In Q2, gross margin was 30%, flat year-on-year, and slightly below Q1, 30.8%, as we began absorbing part of the input cost increase in SOC and memory. I want to highlight clearly about the input cost environment. Critical semiconductor components like SOC, DDR, and flash prices have risen sharply across the electronics industry. Ahead of this, we took strategic positions in SOC and memory, which is why inventory is on the higher side on our balance sheet. This was a planned move, as this protected both our consistent supply situation as well as our margins in the first half year. We strategically passed on the input cost increases rather gradually to market instead of all at once. As market leaders, we did not want to give the channel a sudden price shock and ensured that the momentum of overall market expansion as well as our market share growth continues. As the lower-cost inventories gets used up, and as we moderate price increases to further gain market share, we expect second-half margins to be lower than Q2. Thus, Q2 should not be read as the run rate. That is why we are keeping our full-year EBITDA margin guidance at 14%15%, even after a 16.3% in the first half, so as to keep enough cushion to support our market share growth strategy in such a volatile input cost environment. In September, we completed our qualified institutions placement and raised INR 1,500 crores. Leading domestic and global institutions took part, and I thank them once again for their confidence in the company and management. In October, we repaid all our borrowings, and now the company is debt free. The lower finance cost will show from the second half. The proceeds will go to three users as set out in the placement document: repaying borrowings of Aditya Infotech Limited and also our wholly owned subsidiary, AIL Dixon, manufacturing capacity expansion through our wholly owned subsidiary, inorganic growth through acquisitions, and general corporate purposes. On 30th September, cash and cash equivalents were INR 1,434 crores against borrowing of INR 363 crores. Annualized return on equity for the first half was 26.9%. Also, the finance cost was down 41% year-on-year for first half year. Working capital went up in the half mainly due to increase in inventory because of strategic component buying that I described to maintain supply strength. It was a deliberate choice to secure supply and cost. Collections have improved. Receivable days came down to 87 in Q2 from 93 in Q1 and 105 a year ago. As the components positions get used up, working capital should come down in future. Our board approved, and we signed agreements to invest INR 45.4 crores for a 30% stake on a fully diluted basis in BLUE KGraph AI Solutions. BLUE has built a video compression layer that works on top of standard H.264. It can cut storage and bandwidth by up to 90%, needs no GPU, and works with cameras already installed in the field. The six highly talented co-founders have built and sold a video analytics company already before. For us, this means lower storage costs in our NVRs and cloud, and a recurring software layer on our installed base. We will hold one board seat and one observer seat, and completion is subject to customary conditions. On the manufacturing side, our Kadapa plant capacity is at 2.5 million units a month, and we plan to more than double this through Kadapa phase 3 and a new facility in Greater Noida, funded from the QIP. We are also localizing four components’ categories: lenses, cables, housing enclosures and IoT modules. Our fully integrated lens plant is planned in Greater Noida with a capacity of 2 million lenses per month. The housing and enclosures plant in Kadapa begins trials this quarter, with commercial production coming in place from the coming quarter. The cable plant will also go live in Q4 of this financial year, which is next quarter. Localization improves quality, secures supply, and adds to margins over time as we scale this up. Through our multi brand strategy, we now have a portfolio that addresses every segment of the market, from entry level to the most premium enterprise. CP PLUS is our flagship and mainstream brand. The new range of CP PLUS PRO series targets the premium enterprise segment, and its rollout is planned in the Q4 of FY2027. The other two brands, NEXIVUE and EYRA, serves to the entry and the mid-market, and this counters the lower end of the pyramid as well as the unorganized sector. This festive season, we launched a national campaign with Bollywood superstar Ranveer Singh as our brand ambassador. It ran alongside our presence in the Indian cricket space as well as major airports, and it builds awareness of intelligent security in everyday life. On the back of a strong first half, we are raising our FY2027 guidance. Revenue, we are raising it to INR 6,500 crores, INR 6,800 crores, up from INR 6,000 crores, INR 6,500 crores, which is a growth of 54% to 61% over the year. EBITDA margins, we are maintaining still at 14%-15%, while the PAT margin we are now raising now to 9% to 10%, which earlier was 8.5% to 9.5%. A word on the second half. 90% growth in H1 was partly helped by a soft base last year when STQC certifications backlogs held back supply across the industry. The second half of last year was a normal base, so growth will normalize from these levels while still remaining strong, as you can see in our revised guidance. To sum up, we have widened our market leadership, strengthened our balance sheet, and are investing in capacity, localization, and technology. Our focus remains on disciplined execution and sustainable long-term value creation for all our stakeholders. With that, I will request the moderator to open the line for questions.
