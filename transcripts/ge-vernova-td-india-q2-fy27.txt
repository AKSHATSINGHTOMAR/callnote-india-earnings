Three key pillars are defining the industry. First is unprecedented grid expansion and modernization. India's energy demand is scaling at a pace that requires aggressive expansion of transmission infrastructure. The integration of large-scale renewable energy, often far from consumption centers, requires robust long-distance high-voltage direct current and alternating current corridors. Second is the digital transformation of the grid. The grid has grown more complex, making advanced automations, real-time smart substations, and predictive maintenance essential to reduce AT&C losses and improve the reliability. GE Vernova is focused on providing the software and digital infrastructure required to manage this complexity. Third, the T&D sector is the backbone of India's energy transition and net zero ambition.
